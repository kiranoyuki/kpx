# User Requirements — Assistant

## What can they do
1. Manage the inventory/ supplies of the day (on-call duty). Can access the vendor information to notify for low supply.
2. Prepare the list of equipment and supplies based on the current patient's treatment procedure
3. Log the current treament plan progress / information based on the doctor's note
4. Can see patient's all information
5. Can update patient's health record - pulse, CBCT image, Xray, eg.
6. Can see the assigned patient treatment calendar
7. Can upload the picture of patient - before and after treatment
8. Can page the doctor / manager for work notification
