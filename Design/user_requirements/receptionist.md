# User Requirements — Receptionist

## What can they do
1. Receive new patient booking based on the available calendar
2. Log new patient health's record
3. Manage the booking calendar - create new, update or cancel appointment
4. Can check the availability of all doctor's calendar
5. Can look up the patient's information - bookings, personal information, current treatment plan, doctor's note
6. Follow up with current patients to remind upcoming appointment, deals, new procedures related to patient's dental health
7. Manage the payment process of the patient
8. Remind patient who has missing payment
