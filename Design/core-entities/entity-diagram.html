Entity Map

The data model behind KPX — 40 entities across eight domains: the FDI tooth map the whole clinical record is built on, plus the five mechanisms that carry the clinic — a stranger becoming a patient, a patient becoming revenue, work becoming pay, a doctor's proposal reaching the manager, and supplies moving off the shelf.

6Diagrams

Clinical & revenue — the patient's path through the clinic: care delivered, then billed and collected.

Operations & payroll — the clinic's internal side: staff time, commission, and money paid out. Manager-only.

01

How a stranger becomes a patient

Before anyone generates revenue they have to become a known, verified person. Three facts move independently — we know who they are, we have verified them, they can log in — and keeping them separate is what lets every intake path run through one table.

02

How a tooth is identified

Dentistry happens to a specific tooth, and usually to specific surfaces of it. KPX uses FDI notation (ISO 3950): two digits, quadrant then position from the midline. 46 is the lower-right first molar. Left and right are always the patient’s.

03

How a patient becomes revenue

The operational spine. TreatmentPlan is the clinical anchor, but the session is where money is recognised — the clinic is paid as each visit's work completes, not when a whole procedure finishes.

04

How work becomes pay

Two very different events — a clinician finishing a session and a receptionist bringing a patient back — converge on a single CommissionEntry record. That convergence is the reason payroll has one calculation path instead of two.

05

What needs the manager

Two doctor-initiated requests route through the same approval gate but land in different places. A manager-set promotion bypasses the gate entirely — that contrast is the whole point of separating the two discount paths.

06

Expected supplies vs. actual consumption

The inventory domain runs two parallel tracks that meet at the item. One says what a procedure should need; the other records what it did use. Keeping them separate is what makes variance visible.

07

Complete entity index

All 40 entities with their foreign keys and state enums — the relational and state-machine surface of the model. Full field-level specifications live in entities.md.

Identity & Access

3 entities

User

Person record

Not a login — a person. Every human has exactly one row: staff, patient, or someone who has only ever booked online. Identity lives here once; the profiles carry no names.

Identity
  • full_name required
  • phone required · dedup key
  • email nullable
  • national_id nullable · unique · CCCD, 12 digits
Foreign keys
  • verified_by → User nullable
Enums
status Provisional · Active · Inactive role Manager · Doctor · Receptionist · Accountant · Assistant · Patient
Invariants
  • Active ⇒ verified_at + verified_by
  • national_id ⇒ 12 digits, unique
Portals — granted by profile, not role
  • PatientProfile ⇒ patient portal
  • StaffProfile Intern|Active ⇒ staff portal
Patient sign-in — no password
  • phone OTP proves possession · preferred
  • name + phone + CCCD proves knowledge

Both routes read columns already here, so credential and OTP storage stays deferred. Provisional people never sign in by either route — an OTP would bypass the in-person check.

StaffProfile

Employment

Employment data only — no name, phone or address; those live on User. employment_status is what gates the staff portal.

Foreign keys
  • user_id → User unique
Fields
  • join_date, end_date
  • specialty, license_number
Enums
employment_status Intern · Active · OnLeave · Departed

No pay fields. Rates live on WageRate, one row per rate ever held — a single column could only hold the current one.

Employment ends here, not on User — so someone who quits stays an active person and may still be a patient. Departure is a state change, never a deletion.

Intern is working and assignable, and reaches the staff portal: the difference from Active is terms, not access. The trainee wage and any reduced commission are expressed with existing fields.

PatientProfile

Care

The care relationship, created on arrival — never at online-booking time. Its existence is what makes someone a patient rather than a provisional booker.

Foreign keys
  • user_id → User required · unique
  • created_by → User
Fields
  • emergency_contact, referral_source

Scheduling

4 entities

Chair

A physical treatment position. Chairs, not doctors, cap the clinic’s throughput — two dentists cannot both work if there is one chair.

Foreign keys
  • chairTypeId → ChairType
Enums
status Available · Maintenance · Retired
Invariant

No two appointments may occupy one chair at overlapping times. That is the whole reason this entity exists — an exclusion constraint in PostgreSQL, an application check in SQLite.

ChairType

Reference

What a chair is equipped to do — Standard, Surgical, Orthodontic, Imaging, Paediatric.

Turns a label into a rule

An implant needs the surgical position; a scale does not. ServiceCategory.required_chair_type_id is nullable, and null means any chair — so the constraint is stated only where it is real.

DoctorSchedule

Recurring or one-off availability blocks the receptionist books against.

Foreign keys

Appointment

A scheduled visit. Points at User, so a Provisional person can hold a booking made before they ever arrived. Also carries the credit for a receptionist's follow-up.

Foreign keys
  • person_id → User not PatientProfile
  • doctorId → StaffProfile
  • assistantId → StaffProfile nullable
  • followedUpBy → User nullable

Enums
type Consultation · Procedure · Followup booking_channel Online · FrontDesk · Phone status Scheduled · Confirmed · InProgress · Completed · Cancelled · NoShow

What was done at a visit is its ProcedureSession rows — a visit routinely covers more than one procedure, which a single FK could never record.

Acceptance is the patient’s gate; SpecialProcedureProposal is the manager’s. Price locks at acceptance — the clinic cannot bill a patient before treatment for a price not yet calculated.

TreatmentFailure

Manager determines

Completed work failed — a veneer cracks two days after fitting. One case file carrying the claim, the fault judgment, the remedy, and the charge back to whoever did the work.

Foreign keys
  • procedureId → TreatmentProcedure stays Completed
  • toothCode → Tooth nullable
  • determinedBy → User manager
Enums
status Reported · UnderReview · Resolved · Rejected fault_attribution ClinicTechnique · MaterialDefect · PatientFactor · Inconclusive remedy Refund · FreeRework · Both · None
Three linked consequences
  • negative InvoiceLine + Payment Out money back
  • remedyForFailureId free rework, bills nothing
  • PayrollAdjustment Debit charged to staff

Free rework earns no commission by arithmetic — commission derives from billable_amount, which is zero. The debit is separate and recovers the real loss, lab remake included.

ProcedureInstruction

Reusable clinical instruction template a doctor authors per procedure type.

Foreign keys
  • createdBy → StaffProfile

ProcedureDecision

Append-only

Every status change a procedure passes through — who decided, when, and why. The clinic’s defensive record.

Foreign keys
  • procedureId → TreatmentProcedure
  • decidedBy → User who recorded it
Fields
  • from_status, to_status, decided_at
  • reason required to decline or skip
  • quoted_amount what the patient was actually told
  • risk_explained for a refusal

Informed refusal is the point. A declined_at field holds only the latest state, and treatment is re-proposed — declining a crown on cost in March then accepting in November would overwrite the March refusal, which is exactly the record needed had the tooth fractured instead.

ProcedureSession

Billing unit

One visit’s work on a procedure — and the unit the clinic is paid at. Replaces TreatmentProgress, which already described itself as a session record.

Foreign keys
  • procedureId → TreatmentProcedure
  • appointmentId → Appointment nullable
  • performedBy → StaffProfile
  • assistantId → StaffProfile nullable
Fields
  • session_number, billable_amount
  • progress_note, vitals, next_step_note
Enums
status Scheduled · Completed · Cancelled

Every procedure has at least one, so the rule has no special cases: one completed session, one invoice line. Staff sit here, not on the procedure: only whoever did the work is paid, and three visits may be worked by different doctors and assistants.

billable_amount is the value of this session’s work, present in both payment modes. Commission follows the work, never the money — prepaid or unpaid, an entry appears only when a session completes.

Dental Charting

3 entities

Tooth

Reference

FDI / ISO 3950 notation. 52 static rows — 32 permanent, 20 primary — seeded once and never edited by users.

Key
  • code PK · 11–48, 51–85
  • quadrant, position the two digits
  • valid_surfaces MIDBL ant · MODBL post
  • universal_code nullable · US interop
Enums
dentition Permanent · Primary tooth_type Incisor · Canine · Premolar · Molar

A table rather than a bare CHECK: the chart UI reads these rows, valid_surfaces validates a filling, and name_vi lets the record read as words.

ToothCondition

A clinical finding on one tooth. The patient’s odontogram is their Active rows.

Foreign keys
  • patient_id → PatientProfile
  • tooth_code → Tooth
  • observed_by → User
Enums
status Active · Monitoring · Resolved · EnteredInError condition_type Caries · Fracture · Filling · Crown · Implant · Missing · Impacted …
Invariant

Append-only. A finding recorded in error is marked EnteredInError, never deleted — a clinical record must show what was believed at the time, and by whom.

A proposal never writes here. Planned work is an overlay drawn from ProcedureTooth; only a completed procedure changes the patient’s actual record.

ProcedureTooth

Which teeth and surfaces a procedure addresses. A junction, because a bridge covers three teeth and a molar accumulates procedures over a decade.

Foreign keys
  • procedure_id → TreatmentProcedure
  • tooth_code → Tooth
  • addresses_condition_id → ToothCondition nullable · the clinical why
Fields
  • surfaces e.g. MOD · canonical order
Enums
role Primary · Abutment · Pontic

The tooth never lives in doctor_note. Free text cannot be counted for billing, drawn on a chart, or audited.

Findings link many-to-many here. A root canal and a crown both carry a row on tooth 46 pointing at the same caries — one finding, two procedures — with no third table needed.

Catalog & Pricing

6 entities

ServiceCategory

The service catalog. isSpecial forces a procedure through manager approval; the two scope enums decide how teeth are validated and billed.

Enums
tooth_scope None · SingleTooth · MultiTooth · Quadrant · Arch · FullMouth pricing_basis PerProcedure · PerTooth · PerSurface · PerQuadrant requires_material_choice bool vat_rate per service — medical ≠ cosmetic required_chair_type_id null = any chair resulting_condition_type Filling · Crown · Implant · Missing …

This changes invoice arithmetic. Subtotal becomes unit price × billable quantity — the ProcedureTooth count, or the surface count. A two-surface filling and a three-surface filling are not the same money.

MaterialOption

A material choice within a service, priced separately. A crown is one clinical service, but zirconia and PFM are not the same money — and the patient chooses.

Foreign keys
  • serviceCategoryId → ServiceCategory
Examples
  • Crown PFM · Zirconia · E-max
  • Implant Osstem · Dentium · Straumann
  • Braces Metal · Ceramic · Aligner

Not separate categories: that would duplicate tooth_scope, pricing_basis and the instruction templates, and double the catalogue with every new brand. Clinical rules on the service, commercial choice here.

PriceList

Time-versioned price per category and material, so historical invoices stay correct.

Foreign keys
  • serviceCategoryId → ServiceCategory
  • materialOptionId → MaterialOption null = base price
  • setBy → User

Unique on (category, material, effective_from). Resolution falls back to the base row, so a new material never requires repricing everything.

Promotion

A voucher code the patient presents at billing — XASH at 10%, off the whole invoice. Reaches the invoice without review.

Billing

3 entities

Invoice

A financial document, no longer tied 1:1 to a plan — a 20-month course bills in stages. Both discount paths converge here.

Foreign keys
  • treatmentPlanId → TreatmentPlan no longer unique
  • patientId → PatientProfile
  • promotionId → Promotion nullable
  • discountProposalId → DiscountProposal nullable
Enums
status Draft · Issued · PartiallyPaid · Paid · Overdue · Voided
Vietnamese legal numbering
  • invoice_serial ký hiệu
  • invoice_number số hóa đơn · sequential
  • vat_total, tax_authority_code

A number is taken on issue, never on Draft, and never reused — a Voided invoice keeps its number, since the gap is exactly what sequential numbering prevents.

InvoiceLine

One billable item, frozen when the invoice is issued. The join between clinical work and money.

Foreign keys
  • invoiceId → Invoice
  • sessionId → ProcedureSession nullable · per-session
  • procedureId → TreatmentProcedure nullable · upfront
Snapshot fields
  • description, tooth_codes, surfaces
  • unit_price, quantity, line_total
  • discount_amount pro-rata share of the invoice discount
  • vat_rate, vat_amount computed AFTER discount
  • credits_line_id negative line reverses a charge

VAT is computed on line_total − discount_amount, never before. An invoice-level discount is allocated across lines pro-rata, because VAT rates differ per service — charging tax on the undiscounted price overcharges the patient and misstates the tax.

Snapshotted because clinical records get amended — that is why ToothCondition has EnteredInError. A live view would let a tooth-number correction silently change a bill already issued and paid.

Payment

An individual transaction. Many may settle one invoice, supporting installments.

Foreign keys
  • invoiceId → Invoice
  • receivedBy → User
Enums
direction In · Out method Cash · BankTransfer · Card · Other

Out is a refund. Paired with a negative InvoiceLine so the invoice balances at zero with both the charge and its reversal visible.
